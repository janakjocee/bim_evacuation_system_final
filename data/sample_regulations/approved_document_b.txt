APPROVED DOCUMENT B (FIRE SAFETY)
Means of Escape - Sample Extract for Testing

2.1 Introduction
Buildings must be designed so that there are adequate means of escape in case of fire.
The means of escape should be sufficient to enable people to reach a place of safety.

2.2 Travel Distance Requirements
2.2.1 The travel distance from any point in a building to the nearest exit should not exceed 45 metres.
2.2.2 For buildings with automatic sprinkler systems, the maximum travel distance may be increased to 60 metres.
2.2.3 In dead-end corridors, the travel distance should not exceed 18 metres.

2.3 Door Requirements
2.3.1 All doors on escape routes shall have a minimum clear opening width of 750mm.
2.3.2 Final exit doors shall have a minimum clear opening width of 1050mm.
2.3.3 Doors shall open in the direction of travel where the occupancy exceeds 50 persons.

2.4 Stair Requirements
2.4.1 Escape stairs shall have a minimum width of 1000mm.
2.4.2 The maximum riser height shall be 190mm.
2.4.3 The minimum tread length shall be 250mm.
2.4.4 Stairs serving more than 50 persons shall have a minimum width of 1200mm.

2.5 Capacity Requirements
2.5.1 The capacity of exits shall be sufficient for the anticipated occupancy.
2.5.2 Exit capacity shall be calculated at 90 persons per minute per metre width of door.
2.5.3 Stair capacity shall be calculated at 60 persons per minute per metre width.

2.6 Occupancy Calculations
2.6.1 Office spaces shall be calculated at 0.1 persons per square metre.
2.6.2 Retail spaces shall be calculated at 0.5 persons per square metre.
2.6.3 Corridors shall be calculated at 0.5 persons per square metre.

2.7 Evacuation Time
2.7.1 The total evacuation time shall not exceed 150 seconds for normal risk buildings.
2.7.2 High risk buildings shall have evacuation times not exceeding 90 seconds.

2.8 Exit Requirements
2.8.1 Every building shall have at least two independent exits.
2.8.2 Buildings with occupancy exceeding 500 persons shall have at least three exits.
2.8.3 Exits shall be distributed as remotely as possible from each other.

2.9 Door Width Requirements
2.9.1 Internal doors on escape routes shall have a minimum width of 750mm.
2.9.2 Final exit doors shall have a minimum width of 1050mm.
2.9.3 Doors to protected stairways shall have a minimum width of 800mm.

2.10 Corridor Requirements
2.10.1 Escape corridors shall have a minimum width of 1200mm.
2.10.2 Dead-end corridors shall not exceed 18 metres in length.
2.10.3 Corridors serving wheelchairs shall have a minimum width of 1800mm.

2.11 Visibility
2.11.1 Escape routes shall be clearly signed.
2.11.2 Exit signs shall be illuminated.
2.11.3 Emergency lighting shall be provided on all escape routes.

2.12 Fire Resistance
2.12.1 Escape routes shall have appropriate fire resistance.
2.12.2 Protected stairways shall have 30-minute fire resistance.
2.12.3 Protected corridors shall have 30-minute fire resistance.
